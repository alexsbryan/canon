# Maple House Charter, Article I — Overnight Guests

Members may host overnight guests in their own private rooms. Any single
guest may stay no more than two consecutive nights within any seven-day
period, so that no guest gradually becomes an unofficial resident of the
house. Whenever a guest is in the shared common areas, the hosting member
must accompany them. Each member is fully responsible for the conduct of
their guests for the entire duration of the visit.

# Maple House Charter, Article II — Quiet Hours

Quiet hours are observed every night, running from 11:00 PM until 7:00 AM
the following morning. During quiet hours, conversation in the common areas
must be kept low, and any music, television, or video games must be played
through headphones. A member who anticipates unavoidable late-night noise
is required to give the rest of the house advance notice so that everyone
can reliably sleep.

# Maple House Charter, Article III — Kitchen Cleanup

The kitchen must be cleaned by whoever used it, immediately after that
person finishes cooking or eating. Cleaning means washing every dish you
used, wiping down the counters and the stovetop, and taking out the trash
when the bin is full. Leaving dirty dishes in the sink overnight is never
permitted, no matter how late the hour or how tired the cook.

# Maple House Charter, Article IV — Common Spaces

The living room, the dining room, and the back porch are common areas that
belong equally to every member of the house. No member may reserve a common
area for their own exclusive use, even temporarily, and no member may treat
a common area as a personal extension of their bedroom. These shared spaces
must remain open and available to all members at all times.

# Maple House Charter, Article V — Shared Chores

All members share the household chores equally, following the rotating
weekly schedule posted in the kitchen. Every member is assigned the same
number of chore slots each month, and no member is exempt from the rotation
for any reason whatsoever. A member who cannot complete an assigned chore is
required to arrange a direct swap with another member ahead of time.

# Maple House Charter, Article VI — Pets

No member may keep a pet of any kind in the house, with the single exception
of fish kept in a tank no larger than twenty gallons. This rule exists to
protect members who have allergies and to avoid noise, odor, and damage to
the shared furnishings. A member who wishes to pet-sit an animal for someone
outside the house must obtain unanimous consent from all members first.

# Maple House Charter, Article VII — Smoking

Smoking of any kind is forbidden everywhere inside the house, including
inside a member's own private bedroom. The prohibition covers cigarettes,
cigars, pipes, and vaporizers alike. It exists to keep the shared air clean
and to reduce the risk of fire. Each member is responsible for ensuring that
their visitors also refrain from smoking indoors during any visit.

# Maple House Charter, Article VIII — Rent and Dues

Each member must pay their share of the rent on or before the first day of
every month by transferring it to the house account. The monthly house dues
that cover shared cleaning supplies and paper goods are due on that same day.
A member who anticipates any difficulty paying on time is required to notify
the house treasurer before the first of the month.

# Maple House Charter, Article IX — Personal Belongings

Members must keep their personal belongings in their own bedrooms or in
their assigned storage space. A personal item left in a common area for more
than one full day may be moved by any other member to the owner's bedroom
door. Perishable personal food left out in a common area may be thrown away
after a day. This keeps the shared spaces usable for everyone.

# Maple House Charter, Article X — House Decisions

The house meets on the first Sunday of every month to make decisions
together. A decision passes by a simple majority of the members present, and
a recorded decision amends or extends this Charter from the date it is made.
Any member may place an item on the meeting agenda by posting it on the
kitchen board at least two days before the meeting.

# Decision — 2026-01-12 — Guest Logbook

To keep track of who is in the house at any time, the members resolved that
every overnight guest must be signed into the guest logbook kept by the
front door, recording the guest's name and the dates of their stay. The
hosting member is responsible for making the entry before the guest's first
night. This record is meant to help with safety and accountability.

# Decision — 2026-02-10 — Weeknight Quiet Hours

After repeated complaints about noise on work nights, the house met and
resolved to move the start of quiet hours earlier during the week. Effective
immediately, quiet hours now begin at 10:00 PM on Sunday through Thursday
nights, a full hour earlier than the Charter originally set. The 7:00 AM end
time and the Friday and Saturday start times are left unchanged.

# Decision — 2026-02-24 — Backyard Quiet

Following complaints about late gatherings outdoors, the house resolved that
the quiet-hours rule also applies to the backyard and the back porch, not
only to the indoor common areas. During quiet hours, members and any guests
must keep outdoor conversation low and must not run outdoor speakers. This
decision extends the existing quiet hours rather than changing their times.

# Decision — 2026-03-14 — Guest Policy Revisited

Following a long discussion about privacy, crowding, and the shared cost of
utilities, the house resolved to stop allowing overnight guests altogether.
Effective this month, overnight guests are no longer permitted in the house
at any time, for any number of nights, in any bedroom. Members may still
host visitors during the daytime, but every visitor must leave by 10:00 PM.

# Decision — 2026-03-28 — Guest Parking

To settle ongoing confusion about where visitors should leave their cars,
the house resolved to set aside the two marked spaces at the far end of the
driveway specifically for visitor and guest parking. Guests are welcome to
leave a vehicle parked in those two spaces overnight without needing any
permit. This decision is strictly about parking and changes nothing about
whether or how long guests may stay in the house.

# Decision — 2026-04-02 — Cooking and Cleanup

Recognizing that whichever member cooks dinner for the whole house already
contributes a great deal of effort, the house resolved that whoever cooks
the shared house dinner is excused from cleaning the kitchen on that night.
On those evenings, the work of washing the dishes, wiping the counters, and
taking out the trash falls instead to the members who sat down and ate.

# Decision — 2026-04-18 — Fridge Labeling

To cut down on food going to waste and on disputes over groceries, the house
resolved that members must label their personal food in the shared
refrigerator with their name and the date it was placed there. Any unlabeled
food in the fridge may be treated as communal and eaten by anyone in the
house. This decision adds to the kitchen rules rather than replacing them.

# Decision — 2026-05-06 — Dining Room Reservations

To make room for the occasional birthday or study group, the house resolved
that a member may reserve the dining room for their own private event up to
twice a month by signing the shared calendar at least three days ahead. For
the booked hours of a reserved event, the dining room is closed to the other
members so that the host can use the space.

# Decision — 2026-05-20 — Travel Chore Exemption

Recognizing that several members travel frequently for work, the house
resolved that any member who is away from the house for more than two
consecutive weeks is fully exempt from the chore rotation for the entire
period they are gone. The remaining members will quietly cover the absent
member's chore slots, and the absent member owes no make-up chores on return.

# Decision — 2026-06-03 — House Cat

After a heartfelt request and a long debate, the house resolved by majority
vote to allow one house cat, to be adopted and cared for collectively by the
members. The cat is permitted only if every current member gives written
approval beforehand, and it must be kept out of the bedroom of any member who
objects on the grounds of allergies.

# Decision — 2026-06-10 — Porch Smoking

In response to requests from members who smoke, the house resolved that
smoking is now permitted outdoors on the back porch only, provided that the
smoker uses the sand bucket and sits at least ten feet away from any open
window. Smoking of every kind remains completely forbidden everywhere inside
the house, exactly as before.

# Decision — 2026-06-15 — Late Rent Fee

To encourage everyone to pay on time, the house resolved that a member whose
rent share has not arrived in the house account by the fifth day of the
month owes an additional late fee of twenty-five dollars on top of the rent.
The treasurer will send a reminder to all members two days before the
monthly deadline.

# Maple House Charter, Article XI — Quiet Study Hours

The dining room is set aside as a silent study space every weeknight from
7:00 PM until 9:00 PM. During those hours the dining room is reserved for
quiet individual work and may not be used for meals, conversation, or
gatherings. Members who need to eat during that window should use the
kitchen or their own rooms so that the study hours stay undisturbed for
those who are working.

# Decision — 2026-06-22 — Thursday Movie Nights

To give the house a shared social evening, the members resolved to hold a
movie night in the living room on Thursday evenings. On those nights the
film and the conversation around it may run in the living room until
midnight, and any member who wishes to take part is welcome to stay up that
late. Members who would rather have quiet on a Thursday should plan around
the movie night.
